# ChromAtlas — Logo brief (fast track, assumptions from the codebase)

**Name:** ChromAtlas (one word, capital C and A)
**What it is:** iOS/iPadOS app that indexes your photo library on-device and rebuilds it as an atlas:
a 3D globe of where you've been, an OKLCh colour wheel to search by mood/hue, trips, timeline,
golden-hour / blue-hour filters, shareable summary cards.
**Promise:** "Your camera roll, as an atlas of places and light." Private — no network, no account.
**Audience:** travellers and photographers with large libraries; design-literate iPhone users.
**Adjectives:** luminous · precise · private · wandering · cinematic
**Existing equity:** dark UI (#0C0E12), amber (#FFB861) and cyan (#73D9FF) accents, rounded heavy caps
"CHROMATLAS" with wide tracking on the share card.
**Must work:** iOS app icon, 16–32 px, share-card header, App Store, one colour, on dark.

## Category clichés (avoid or twist)
- Photo apps: multi-colour radial pinwheels/petals, camera apertures, lenses.
- Travel/maps: map pins, globes with lat/long grids, compasses, paper planes.

## Word map → intersections
places × light → the day/night line on Earth (the terminator), sunrise on a horizon
atlas × colour → a colour atlas (book of swatches) ↔ a folded map; panels as hues
private × atlas → a pocket map, folded and closed

## Longlist (one line each)
1. Terminator — a globe split by the curved day/night line, where golden and blue hour happen. ★
2. Folded atlas — an accordion-folded map whose three panels are three hues, with one place punched through. ★
3. Horizon A — the A of Atlas whose crossbar is a horizon with the sun rising in its counter. ★
4. Hue meridians — a globe whose meridians are hue bands (too close to photo-app pinwheels).
5. Chroma pin — a map pin cut from a colour wheel (pin cliché).
6. Passport stamp — circular stamp with swatch (emblem, too busy for 16 px).
7. Swatch globe — a paint chip whose corner curls into a sphere (two ideas at once).
8. C-orbit — a C as an orbit arc around one dot (generic).
9. Aperture latitudes — aperture blades as latitude lines (aperture cliché).
10. Fold wordmark — the two A's in the wordmark drawn as folded panels (wordmark-only, weak as icon).
